What this is

Every time this page opens (and every 10 minutes while it stays open) it fetches live prices for the most active Polymarket Yes/No markets. For each one it looks for the same question on Kalshi, PredictIt and Manifold, averages what they say (real-money sites count more than play-money Manifold), and moves Polymarket's price only part of the way toward that average. The result is PolyLean's chance for Yes, and the chance for No is the rest.

Chance, edge and actions

  • Chance is shown on every market: the chance it happens (Yes) and the chance it doesn't (No).
  • Minimum chance (default 70%) is the least chance the side you would buy must have. Raising it removes actions; lowering it can only add them.
  • Action means two things at once: the side has at least your minimum chance, and the price you would really pay (the ask for Yes, or 1 minus the bid for No) is at least 6 points below that chance.
  • High chance, no edge lists markets that are very likely but already priced that way. Buying them wins a few cents and risks the whole stake, so the app says no trade.
  • Watch means the model sees an edge, but on a side with less than your minimum chance.

Why a high chance alone is not enough

An 85% chance bought at 86¢ is a bad bet: you win 16¢ per $1 and lose the whole $1 about 15% of the time. A bet is only attractive when the chance is clearly above the price. That is why most high-chance markets say no trade.

The stake size

If you enter a bankroll, the stake is a quarter of the Kelly amount, capped at 5% of your bankroll per market. It assumes the model's chance is right, which it may not be, so treat it as a ceiling, not a target.

The scorecard is the product

Prediction-market prices already hold most public information, so every lean is only a hypothesis. Each lean is saved when it first appears and scored when the market resolves. If the model's Brier score is not lower than the plain market's, the model adds nothing. Wait for about 30 resolved leans before reading anything into it.

Limits

  • The chance is PolyLean's estimate, not a fact. With no outside forecast it is just the market's own price.
  • The scorecard is stored in this browser on this device and only grows while the page is open. Use Download backup.
  • Only two outside sources. Similar wording can hide different resolution rules, so read Polymarket's rules before trading.
  • It does not read blogs, news or social media.
Information only, not financial advice. Prediction markets carry a real risk of losing everything you stake, and rules differ by country. Check that it is allowed where you live before you trade.
