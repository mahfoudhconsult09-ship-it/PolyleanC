What this is

Every time this page opens (and every 10 minutes while it stays open) it fetches live prices for the most active Polymarket Yes/No markets. For each one it looks for the same question on Kalshi and Manifold, averages what they say, and moves Polymarket's price only part of the way toward that average. It then compares the result with what you would really pay: the ask for Yes, or the bid for No.

Yes, No or Skip

  • Yes / No: the adjusted probability beats the real price by at least 6 points after the spread.
  • Skip: no matching question, sources agree with the market, the price is near 0% or 100%, volume is thin, or the market closes within an hour. Most markets are Skip, on purpose.
  • Questions only match when their numbers and years are identical, so "above $90k" is never compared with "above $100k".

The scorecard is the product

Prediction-market prices already hold most public information, so a lean is only a hypothesis. Each lean is saved when it first appears and scored when the market resolves. If the model's Brier score is not lower than the plain market's, the model adds nothing. Wait for about 30 resolved leans before reading anything into it.

Limits

  • The scorecard is stored in this browser on this device, and only grows while the page is open. Clearing browser data erases it. Use Download backup.
  • Only two outside sources. Many markets have no match.
  • Matching is word-based. Similar wording can hide different resolution rules, so read Polymarket's rules before trading.
  • It does not read blogs, news or social media.
